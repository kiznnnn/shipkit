Ship Kit

Privacy

Short version: the plugin runs on your machine and sends nothing to us. This site counts visits without cookies or IP addresses.

The plugin

Ship Kit has no server. The commands run inside your own Claude Code session, and everything they write stays in .shipkit/ in your project. Its scripts only send GET requests: to your own public site and to the links on its page, such as your checkout, to see that they open (only those links when the check reads local files), and to public sources such as Google autocomplete and Hacker News search when a Pro command researches keywords or people. Your repo, your posts and your numbers never come to us.

Claude Code itself talks to Anthropic as it always does. That's between you and Anthropic, under their terms, and Ship Kit doesn't change it.

This website

  • A small counter of our own records page views, free web check runs, and clicks on the GitHub and buy buttons. For each, it keeps the time, the page path, the referring site's host name (for example t.co), which button was clicked, and your country. It stores no IP address, no cookie and no user id.
  • Add ?nostats to any page address once and this browser is left out from then on.
  • The free web check sends the address you type to our checker, which fetches that public page, its share image and its robots.txt, and returns the result. Results are cached for 5 minutes so the same page isn't fetched twice.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts.
