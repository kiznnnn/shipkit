      09 · Questions

      Fair questions.

      Does it post for me?

      No. It never posts, sends, submits, deploys or signs up for anything. It writes files in .shipkit/ and you post from your own accounts. That keeps your accounts safe and the posts yours.

      Will it make things up?

      It's built not to. Every fact goes into .shipkit/product.md with its source, and pages and posts may only use those facts. Anything missing, like a quote or a number, becomes a [needs: …] slot for you.

      What do I need?

      Claude Code on any plan, and Node 18 or newer. The scripts have no dependencies and only use free public sources. There's nothing else to pay for.

      Will Reddit remove an AI-written post?

      Some subreddits will: many now remove AI-written posts, and a few ban promoting content tools at all. Ship Kit tells you which ones, from rules it dates and re-checks. It never tries to make AI text pass as yours. It drafts, then asks you to put it in your own words where a community requires it.

      Is it only for Mac apps?

      No. It was tested on a Mac app first, but it works on any repo: web apps, SaaS, CLIs, libraries, plugins and mobile apps.

      Why not use a free skill pack?

      Use them; they're good libraries of frameworks. Ship Kit is narrower. It looks at your actual repo and live site, follows each platform's current rules, and hands you a finished, dated launch.

      How does the price ladder work?

      Copies 1 to 25 are $9, or more if you want to pay more. Copies 26 to 75 are $19. After that it's $29. The counter on this page shows where it stands.

      Can I use it for clients?

      Yes. Pro covers every product you make, client work included. If several people on a team use it, Studio covers five.

      What if it's not for me?

      Reply to your Gumroad receipt within 14 days for a full refund. No questions.
